About Limitime

Limitime connects to compatible heated-tobacco devices via Bluetooth and helps you monitor your usage and reduce smoking by using features such as automatic recording and device locking.

If You Cannot Connect via Bluetooth

Please check the following:

  • Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone.
  • Limitime has permission to use Bluetooth.
  • Your compatible device is powered on.
  • Your device is in a state where Bluetooth connection is available.
  • Your device is not currently connected to another smartphone or app.

If you still cannot connect, close Limitime, check the Bluetooth and device status, then open the app and try again.

Usage Records

Limitime records usage based on information obtained from compatible devices. Depending on Bluetooth connectivity and iOS behaviour, records may occasionally take some time to appear in the app.

Device Locking

For supported devices, Limitime can perform lock and unlock operations from the app.

Depending on Bluetooth connectivity and the current device state, an operation may not be reflected immediately. Please check the status shown in Limitime after performing an operation.

Supported Environment

Limitime is an iPhone app. Please check the latest App Store listing for currently supported iOS versions and compatible devices.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete user data stored by Limitime from the Settings screen in the app. The deletion process requires a network connection and cannot be undone.
